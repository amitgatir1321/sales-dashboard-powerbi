# Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
This project analyzes the Sample - Superstore sales dataset and presents the results through an interactive Power BI dashboard. The analysis focuses on sales performance, profitability, regional trends, category performance, and top-selling products.

## Dataset
- **Dataset:** Sample - Superstore
- **Records:** 9,994
- **Distinct Orders:** 5,009
- **Key fields:** Order Date, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Category, Region, Segment, Product Name

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Excel
- DAX

## Dashboard Components
- KPI cards for Sales, Profit, Orders, Quantity, and Profit Margin
- Sales Trend by Year
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Profit by Category
- Interactive filters for Region, Category, and Segment

## Key Insights
1. **Technology** recorded the highest category sales at $836,154.03 and the highest category profit at $145,454.95.
2. **Furniture** generated $741,999.80 in sales but only $18,451.27 in profit, making its profitability an area for investigation.
3. **West** was the highest-sales region with $725,457.82 in sales and $108,418.45 in profit.
4. **South** recorded the lowest regional sales at $391,721.91.
5. Sales reached their highest annual level in **2017**, at $733,215.26.
6. **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the top-selling product, generating $61,599.82 in sales.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing Technology products while maintaining appropriate inventory levels.
- Investigate pricing, discounting, and product-level profitability within Furniture.
- Study the product and customer mix contributing to strong West-region performance.
- Analyze the causes of the 2015 decline and drivers behind growth from 2016 to 2017.
- Use dashboard filters to monitor performance across regions, categories, and segments.
